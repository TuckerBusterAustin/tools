Questions

Does Speedbrief exist yet?

No. It is coming soon. This page is a test to see if people want it. Joining the waitlist helps us decide whether to build it.

Who is it for?

Solo owners of small Shopify stores who are not developers. It is also for freelancers who build and fix stores for them.

How is it different from PageSpeed Insights?

PageSpeed Insights is free and shows scores and a list of diagnostics. Speedbrief will aim to turn that into an ordered list of fixes for a Shopify store, in plain words, with steps to make each one safely. You can still use PageSpeed Insights too.

Shopify already has a Speed report. How is this different?

As we understand it, Shopify's report shows a speed score and how it changes over time. Speedbrief will aim to list specific fixes in order, such as which apps or images to look at first. We have not tested this yet, so treat it as our plan.

Will it make my store rank higher on Google?

We can't promise that. Speed is one signal among many. Content and relevance matter too. Speedbrief will aim to show you what to fix, not guarantee a result.

Will it change my store?

No. It will give you a report with steps. You decide what to do and when.

Is it a subscription?

No. The plan is a one-time report. See the pricing section on this page for the options.

When will it launch? Can I pay now?

We don't have a launch date yet. Joining the waitlist does not take any payment. If we open pre-orders, the checkout will show the delivery date and the refund terms before you pay. Otherwise we will email you when it launches.
