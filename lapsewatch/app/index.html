    Questions

    Can I use Lapsewatch today?

    No. Lapsewatch is not built yet. You can join the waitlist, and we'll email you once at launch. The free checker works today with expiry dates you enter yourself.

    Will Lapsewatch renew my certificates?

    No. It will only remind you. Renew the certificate yourself or ask your host.

    How will the alerts work?

    Email and Slack alerts at 30, 14 and 7 days are coming; they are not built yet. Version 1 sends nothing. It gives you reminder text to paste and a calendar file (.ics) for Pro.

    How many domains will it handle?

    It is planned for about 10 to 100 client domains. The free checker takes up to 50 at a time.

    What will it cost?

    Freelancer $9/mo, Agency $19/mo, or Agency Annual $182/yr. Nothing is charged now.

    Is the free checker live?

    Partly. The checker on this page and on its own page works today: you enter each expiry date and it sorts the days left. Reading certificates for you is coming and will need a server.

    What happens to my email address?

    We will use it once, to tell you Lapsewatch has launched. Read the privacy note when it is published.

    Which certificates will it check?

    Public HTTPS sites on port 443. Domains that don't answer will show as "Couldn't check", with a reason.
