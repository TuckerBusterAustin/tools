Questions

What is free?

The expiry checker is free. Paste your domains and dates, and get a sorted days-left table. You can also save up to 5 domains in the app.

What does paying unlock?

Pro raises the limit from 5 to 100 saved domains. It adds a calendar file with reminders at 30, 14 and 7 days, copy-ready Slack and email reminder text, and CSV import and export.

How does Lapsewatch get the expiry dates?

You enter them. A web page cannot read a site's certificate by itself. You can find a date in your browser's certificate viewer, or with an openssl command. The checker accepts dates like 2026-12-01 and the openssl 'notAfter' format.

Does it send me emails or Slack messages on its own?

No. Lapsewatch gives you a calendar file and reminder text you can paste into Slack or email. Your calendar then does the reminding.

Where does my data go?

Your saved list stays in your own browser. The free checker stores nothing and sends nothing. Clearing your browser data removes the saved list, so export a CSV to keep a copy (Pro).

Will it renew my certificates?

No. Lapsewatch only reminds you, so you can renew the certificate yourself or ask your host.
