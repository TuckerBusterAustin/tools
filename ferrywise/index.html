Ferrywise is in development.

One flat price. No credits, no runtime meter.

Ferrywise is a browser extension that is coming. It will copy client details from one website into a form on another, and fill your repeat forms, without you building a flow. It is not built yet. Join the waitlist to tell us what you need.

The problem

  • You type the same client details into the same web forms every day.
  • You copy data from one website and paste it into another, again and again.
  • Free macro tools can work, but setting them up takes effort.
  • Recorded macros may break when a site changes its layout.
  • Some cloud automation tools charge by credits or usage, which can make the bill hard to plan for.
  • You are not a developer. You just want the repeat task done.

What it will do

  • Ferrywise will be a browser extension you add to your browser.
  • It will copy client details from one site into a form on another.
  • It will fill in forms you use again and again, so you stop retyping.
  • It will be set up without building flows or writing code.
  • Paid plans are planned at a flat monthly price, with no credit or runtime meter. Pricing is shown below.
  • The first version is planned to cover cross-site copy and form filling only. Anything more is not promised.

FAQ

Can I use Ferrywise today?
No. Ferrywise is not built yet. This page is to find out if the idea is useful. Joining the waitlist is how you tell us it is.
Who is it for?
Freelance virtual assistants and small-agency ops or account-management staff. It is for people who enter the same client details into the same few web tools every day, and who are not developers.
What will the first version do?
It will copy client details from one website into a form on another, and fill forms you repeat. That is the plan. Plans can change based on what people tell us.
How is this different from free tools like Automa or UI.Vision?
Those tools are free and can do a lot. We are testing whether people want something with less setup and a flat price. We do not know yet. Your answer helps us find out.
How will pricing work?
The plan is a flat monthly price with no credit or runtime meter. The planned tiers are shown on this page. Nothing is for sale yet, and prices could change before launch.
Do I need a credit card to join the waitlist?
No. Joining the waitlist only needs your email address.
