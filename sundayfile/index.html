Sundayfile is in development.

Coming soon: a clean CSV of your HubSpot and Trello data, in your Drive every week

Sundayfile is coming soon. It will send a weekly CSV of your HubSpot deals and contacts and your Trello boards to Google Drive. Same columns every time. One folder per client. Nobody has to remember.

The problem

  • Getting a regular CSV out of HubSpot usually means someone has to remember to export it.
  • Exporting from Trello is a manual step, and Trello's CSV export is limited to paid plans.
  • Someone on your team has to export, clean up and merge the files by hand.
  • Clients and leadership ask for the same report again and again, and the columns keep changing.
  • Automation tools and backup apps exist, but you may have to set up and maintain the file format yourself, and some charge by usage.

What it will do

  • Sundayfile will export your HubSpot deals and contacts to CSV once a week.
  • It will export your Trello boards to CSV once a week.
  • Files will land in your Google Drive on their own, in one folder per client.
  • Columns will be the same every time, so your sheets and reports do not break.
  • Setup will be one short flow: connect your accounts, pick a Drive folder, done.
  • Pricing will be flat per client workspace. No task counting. A workspace means one client: one HubSpot account and one Trello workspace.
  • At launch it will cover HubSpot and Trello only, with weekly timing and basic formatting.

FAQ

Can I use Sundayfile today?
No. Sundayfile is not built yet. It is coming soon, if enough teams want it. This page is a test. Joining the waitlist helps us decide whether to build it.
Which tools will it work with?
HubSpot and Trello are planned for launch. We may look at more later, but we are not promising any.
What is a client workspace?
One client. That means one HubSpot account and one Trello workspace. Each plan will cover a set number of client workspaces, shown in the plans on this page.
How often will it export?
Once a week. Daily or custom schedules are not planned for the first version.
Where will the files go?
To a Google Drive folder you choose, with one folder per client.
Is this a full backup of HubSpot or Trello?
No. It will give you CSV files of HubSpot deals and contacts and of your Trello boards. It will not copy everything in your accounts.
Other tools can export HubSpot or Trello data. How is this different?
That is true, and we do not claim otherwise. Sundayfile is planned to be narrower: HubSpot and Trello together, in one fixed set of columns, one folder per client, with flat pricing per client workspace. We have not tested whether that is enough of a reason to switch.
How much will it cost?
The plans and prices are shown on this page. Pricing will be flat per client workspace, not per task. Prices may change before launch.
Do I pay anything now?
No. Joining the waitlist is free and we take no payment. If we later offer a way to reserve a spot, it will be optional, and we will say clearly that the product is not built yet, when any charge happens, and how refunds work.
What if you never build it?
Then we will tell you by email and you will owe nothing.
