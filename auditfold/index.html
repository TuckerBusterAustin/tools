What it does

Page audit: Auditfold will check the page you have open

That includes meta tags, headings and internal links.

Fix list: it will turn the results into a short list in plain words, ranked by what matters most.

Shareable report: you will be able to send the fix list to a client as a simple report.

Built for one page at a time: it will work on the page in your browser, not crawl a whole site.

Under consideration: a basic backlink summary

We have not decided yet. Tell us if you would need it.

Not planned for the first version: site-wide crawling and domain authority scores.

Questions

Can I use Auditfold today?

No. Auditfold is not built yet. This page is a test to see if people want it. Joining the waitlist helps us decide whether to build it.

Who is it for?

Solo SEO consultants and small agencies with 1 to 5 people who sell site or page audits to small-business clients.

Will it crawl my client's whole site?

No. It will check the page you have open in your browser. Site-wide crawling is not planned for the first version.

Will it show domain authority?

No. Domain authority scores are not planned for the first version.

Will it include backlink data?

We are not sure yet. Backlink data usually comes from a paid data source, and we have not checked the cost. If you would need it, tell us when you join.

How much will it cost?

See the plans on this page. These are planned prices and can still change before launch.

What is the Reserve button?

It is a way to tell us you would pay for this at the listed price. It only takes your email. You do not pay anything and you are not signed up for a charge.

What does joining the waitlist mean?

You give us your email. We will write to you about the launch and may ask you one or two short questions. You do not have to buy anything.
