Questions

What is free?

The full on-page audit is free. You get the score, the ranked fix list, the sample page and copy-as-text. It does not ask for a license.

What does Pro unlock?

Two things. The client report export, a standalone HTML report with your name and your client's name that you can print or send. And saved history, where you save audits, reopen them and delete them.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Auditfold makes no network calls. The HTML you paste is checked in your browser. Saved history is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device.

Does it crawl my client's whole site or fetch a URL?

No. You paste the HTML of one page and it audits that page. It does not fetch URLs or crawl sites.

Does it show backlinks or domain authority?

No. It covers on-page checks only. It does not show backlink data or domain authority scores.

Does the report need my client's name or any setup?

You type your name and your client's name into two boxes. The report is then built as a single HTML file you download. It has no external links or files.
