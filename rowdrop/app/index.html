The Monday export, by hand

  • You open HubSpot, find the same list, and click export. Again.
  • You rename the file and attach it to an email.
  • Your client has no HubSpot login, so you are the only way they get the data.
  • Some reports can’t be emailed as files at all.
  • When you are on holiday or busy, the file simply doesn’t go out.
  • Nobody wants a new data platform just to fix this.

How it will work

  1. 1

    Pick a list or report

    Choose what you already use in HubSpot, and CSV or Excel.

  2. 2

    Pick a schedule

    Daily, weekly or monthly, at a time and time zone you choose.

  3. 3

    Name who gets the file

    Type email addresses. Anyone works, with or without HubSpot.

What it will do

  • Rowdrop will send a HubSpot list or report as a CSV or Excel file.
  • You will pick a daily, weekly or monthly schedule.
  • Files will go to anyone you name by email, including people with no HubSpot login.
  • It will also cover lists and report types that HubSpot can’t email as files.
  • Setup will take a few clicks. No code and no spreadsheet setup.
  • Rowdrop will work with HubSpot only.

Questions

Can I use Rowdrop today?

Partly. A browser-only preview is available now (CSV import, schedule reminders, one-click file build). The live HubSpot connection and email sending are coming soon. The waitlist form only notes your interest in this browser for now, because there is no server yet, so we cannot email you.

What will it send?

A HubSpot list or report, as a CSV or Excel file.

Do recipients need a HubSpot login?

No. Any email address will work.

How often can files go out?

Daily, weekly or monthly, at the time and time zone you pick.

Will it change anything in HubSpot?

No. It will only read your lists and reports.

What if a file fails to send?

You will see it marked Failed with a plain reason, and you can retry. Missed-run alerts are in-app now; email alerts are coming soon.

Will it work with other tools?

No. Rowdrop will work with HubSpot only.

Will I be charged for joining the waitlist?

No. Prices may change before launch, and reserving only saves your interest.
