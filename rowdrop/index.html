Rowdrop is in development.

Coming soon: your Monday HubSpot export, sent on schedule.

Rowdrop is not built yet. It will send a HubSpot list or report as a CSV or Excel file, on a schedule you pick, to anyone. That includes clients with no HubSpot login. No code. No spreadsheet setup.

The problem

  • You send the same HubSpot report to Excel every Monday, by hand.
  • HubSpot can email some reports as files on a schedule, but not every report type.
  • Those scheduled emails only go to people with a HubSpot login. Your client has none.
  • Your boss or client wants the same spreadsheet every week or month.
  • Big data platforms cost too much when you only need one file a week.
  • Scripts and macros break, and no one notices until the file is missing.

What it will do

  • Rowdrop will send a HubSpot list or report as a CSV or Excel file.
  • You will pick a daily, weekly or monthly schedule.
  • Files will go to anyone you name by email, including people with no HubSpot login.
  • It will also cover lists and report types that HubSpot can't email as files.
  • Setup will take a few clicks. No code and no spreadsheet setup.
  • Rowdrop will work with HubSpot only.

FAQ

Is Rowdrop available now?
No. Rowdrop is not built yet. This page is a test to see if people want it. Joining the waitlist helps us decide whether to build it.
What will Rowdrop do?
It will send a HubSpot list or report as a CSV or Excel file on a daily, weekly or monthly schedule. The file can go to anyone, even people with no HubSpot login.
Doesn't HubSpot already email reports?
HubSpot can email some report types as files on a schedule. Not every report type is covered, and the emails go only to people with a HubSpot login. Rowdrop is coming to fill those gaps.
Which tools will it work with?
Rowdrop will work with HubSpot only.
Where will the file go?
The first version will send files by email. Other places, like a shared folder, are not planned yet.
Do I need to write code?
No. You will pick a list or report, a schedule and a file type.
How much will it cost?
The planned prices are shown on this page. They may change before launch. Pressing a price button only saves your interest. You will not be charged.
Who is it for?
People in marketing ops, RevOps or reporting at small and mid-sized companies. They send the same HubSpot data to a boss, a client or a spreadsheet every week or month.
Is Rowdrop part of HubSpot?
No. Rowdrop is not affiliated with or endorsed by HubSpot. HubSpot is a trademark of HubSpot, Inc.
